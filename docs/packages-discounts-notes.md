What's actually discounted

1. The room — ₹500 off every night. Standard ₹5,500 / ₹7,500 / ₹9,500 becomes ₹5,000 / ₹7,000 / ₹9,000 inside the package. Same flat ₹500 in every season.

2. The length discount — ₹2,000 at 5 nights, ₹4,000 at 7. A lump sum off the total, not per night. Nothing at 3 nights.

3. The massage — but only as a value, never as a rate. The package carries ₹2,500 per treatment against a menu that starts at ₹2,900. That's the piece we deliberately never publish, because a guest choosing the ₹2,900 Abhyangam would otherwise be able to work out they'd embedded ₹2,500.

What is not discounted

Yoga and breakfast are charged at ₹1,000 per person per day, and that number is already below menu. Yoga is ₹450 for two classes against a ₹600 drop-in — so a guest attending both pays ₹450 for what would cost ₹1,200. Breakfast is ₹550, which I've been treating as roughly your menu price.

So yoga is heavily reduced, but not by a discount you apply on top — it's baked into the ₹1,000 flat rate. There's no separate lever there.

Where the savings actually come from

For a 3-night couple, the ₹6,800 saving breaks down as:

Source Amount
Yoga (₹1,200 → ₹450 pp/day), discount: ₹4,500
Massage (₹2,900 → ₹2,500), discount: ₹800
Room (₹500 × 3 nights), discount: ₹1,500
Breakfast ₹0
Total ₹6,800

Yoga is two-thirds of your entire discount — and it's the one that costs you nothing, since the classes run twice daily whether or not a package guest shows up. The room and massage discounts are the ones with real cost attached, and they're the small ones.
